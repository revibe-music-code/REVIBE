# REVIBE

REVIBE is a music player for Android. It plays YouTube Music, and it plays music files you already keep on your phone or SD card. It is an independent project derived from [InterTune](https://github.com/ItzSkyeYT/InterTune), which in turn comes from [InnerTune](https://github.com/z-huang/InnerTune)/[OuterTune](https://github.com/OuterTune/OuterTune): it started as a copy of that line of apps and then went its own way. The original projects and their creators are credited as the base, and REVIBE is maintained separately from them. REVIBE carries its own fixes, the main one being that YouTube Music songs play all the way through again instead of cutting out after about half a minute. It is one person's project, kept public because other people find it useful. If something is broken, open an issue **in this REVIBE repository**, not in the original project's tracker: upstream is the credit and the starting point, not the place where REVIBE is supported.
